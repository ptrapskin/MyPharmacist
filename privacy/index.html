Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected on mypharmacist.care and the tools it links to, and what patients, caregivers, and healthcare professionals should understand before using them.

Last updated: July 2026

1. Overview

MyPharmacist itself is a simple, static page that links out to tools built for patients, caregivers, and healthcare professionals. Some of those tools are hosted on a separate site (catylstrx.com / tools.catylstrx.com), which has its own privacy policy.

2. Personal and Health Information

Don't enter sensitive personal or health information into a tool unless that tool's own page explains how the information is handled. Where a tool asks for information like a name, address, or medication details (for example, to fill out a donation record), that information is used only to generate the record you asked for and is described in more detail on that tool's page.

3. Information We May Collect

This site may collect limited technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and basic analytics (via Google Analytics) to understand usage and improve the site.

4. How Tool Inputs Are Handled

Tools linked from this page vary in how they handle information you enter. Some run entirely in your browser and never send your inputs anywhere. Others (like a site locator) send only what's needed to answer your request (for example, an address to find nearby locations). Check the individual tool's page for specifics if you're unsure.

5. Cookies and Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics, which may use cookies or similar technologies to understand how the site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers that support hosting, analytics, or security, as applicable.

7. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by this site, but no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: admin@catylstrx.com

9. Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.
